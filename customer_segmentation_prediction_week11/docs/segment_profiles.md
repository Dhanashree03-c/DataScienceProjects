# Customer Segment Profiles

## Overview

Customer segmentation was performed using the **K-Means clustering algorithm** with an optimal cluster size of **three clusters**, determined using the **Elbow Method**.

The clustering process grouped customers with similar behavioral and financial characteristics based on the following features:

* Tenure
* Monthly Charges
* Total Charges
* Contract Type
* Payment Method
* Paperless Billing
* Senior Citizen Status

These segments help the business understand **different customer behaviors and churn risk levels**, allowing for targeted marketing and retention strategies.

# Segment 0 — Loyal High-Value Customers

### Key Characteristics

* **Highest average tenure** among all segments
* **High monthly spending**
* Long-term relationship with the company
* Very **low churn rate (~6.8%)**

### Behavioral Insights

These customers represent the **most stable and loyal portion of the customer base**. They have maintained long-term contracts and consistently generate high revenue.

Their long tenure indicates **high satisfaction with the service**, making them a critical segment for maintaining steady revenue streams.

### Business Importance

* High lifetime value
* Reliable recurring revenue
* Lower acquisition cost compared to new customers

### Recommended Strategy

* Introduce **loyalty rewards or premium service tiers**
* Provide **exclusive offers and early feature access**
* Maintain **excellent customer support**

# Segment 1 — Moderate Spenders

### Key Characteristics

* **Moderate tenure**
* **Lower monthly charges compared to other segments**
* Moderate lifetime spending
* **Moderate churn rate (~11%)**

### Behavioral Insights

Customers in this group show **steady but moderate engagement** with the service. They may not be the highest spenders but still contribute meaningful recurring revenue.

Their moderate churn rate indicates that **price sensitivity or service value perception may influence retention**.

### Business Importance

* Represents a **large portion of the customer base**
* Significant opportunity to **increase lifetime value**

### Recommended Strategy

* Offer **personalized promotions**
* Introduce **bundle packages or discounts**
* Encourage **longer contract commitments**

# Segment 2 — High Monthly Charge Customers

### Key Characteristics

* **Highest monthly charges**
* Moderate tenure
* High total spending
* **Highest churn risk (~14.7%)**

### Behavioral Insights

Customers in this segment are **high spenders but also the most likely to churn**. This may indicate dissatisfaction with pricing or service expectations.

These customers may have **premium plans or higher service usage**, which increases their expectations for reliability and support.

### Business Importance

* High short-term revenue contribution
* Highest risk of revenue loss due to churn

### Recommended Strategy

* Conduct **targeted retention campaigns**
* Offer **personalized discounts or retention incentives**
* Improve **customer support responsiveness**

# Summary of Customer Segments

| Segment   | Description                   | Churn Risk | Business Priority   |
| --------- | ----------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------- |
| Segment 0 | Loyal High-Value Customers    | Low        | Maintain loyalty    |
| Segment 1 | Moderate Spenders             | Medium     | Increase engagement |
| Segment 2 | High Monthly Charge Customers | High       | Focus on retention  |

# Key Insights

* **Tenure is the strongest predictor of customer retention.**
* Customers with **longer relationships show significantly lower churn rates.**
* High-spending customers require **strong retention strategies** due to higher churn risk.
* Segment-based modeling allows the company to **design targeted business strategies instead of generic retention approaches.**

# Business Impact

Applying segmentation-driven strategies can lead to:

* **Improved customer retention**
* **Higher lifetime customer value**
* **More efficient marketing campaigns**
* **Reduced churn-related revenue loss**

Segment-based analytics enables organizations to **focus resources where they have the greatest business impact**.
